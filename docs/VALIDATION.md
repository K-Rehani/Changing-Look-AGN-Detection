# Validation and next observations

The automated tests use deterministic synthetic spectra with a known disappearing broad component, a continuum-only offset control, different wavelength grids, masked line pixels, missing Hα coverage, zero inverse variance, an analytic Gaussian flux/EW check at two redshifts, a miniature DR16Q-like catalog, and FITS round-trip. `clagn demo` creates a visual example. Passing these tests checks the implementation's intended behavior under simplified conditions; it is not a measurement of completeness or purity on SDSS.

For a real survey evaluation, record the catalog checksum and data release; inspect a stratified sample including high and low scores and apparent broad transitions; compile independently vetted changing-look examples and stable controls; compare fits with continuum/host/Fe II alternatives; examine calibration with narrow lines and imaging/aperture information; and estimate recovery, false positives, and selection as a function of S/N, redshift, wavelength coverage, rest-frame time separation, and flux state. Split threshold tuning from final evaluation. Document human-review decisions and disagreements. No survey-scale benchmark or confirmed candidate is included here.
